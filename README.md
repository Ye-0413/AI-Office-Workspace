# AI Work Space

**Observe your agent team collaboration in your spare display**

A local macOS desktop companion for **Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode** and more. Bring real sessions from different AI tools into a shared room, where paper, ink, moss, yarn and warm light make their work visible. See who is reading, building, waiting for a tool or waiting for you—while each session keeps its real identity and helpers.

**5 original companions · 550 motion variations · 28 tool integrations · 6 everyday + 27 holiday rooms**

## Meet the workshop

Five materials, five silhouettes, five ways of paying attention. These portraits describe the **character concepts**, including ideas for signature gestures; the [Motion gallery](#550-ways-to-be-at-work) shows the motions implemented in this release. Their work themes give them personality. Every companion can represent every supported activity, and appearance does not route tasks or change an AI tool's abilities.

### Folio · 纸团 — Writing & research

A cream-white origami creature, shaped like a letter that has not quite finished folding itself. Two uneven paper corners form its ears; four short folded legs hold it up. Its face peeks out from the creases, as if it has always belonged there.

Folio is earnest, sensitive and a little too particular about a clean page. Its movement language is folding, turning and pressing flat: opening gently to write, folding one ear and then the other while thinking. In its character story, a finished piece emerges as one neatly prepared slip of paper—a small reward for getting the details right.

### Ink · 墨豆 — Coding & debugging

A nearly black navy ink drop with the low, flattened shape of a smooth stone that can flow. Four soft feet keep it close to the ground; one fine, luminous eye slit gives it a quietly determined expression.

Quiet, stubborn and quick to act, Ink works with fast little taps and compact changes of shape. Its design imagines a difficult bug flattening it into a small puddle before it gathers itself again. Helpers drip out of the same ink and merge back into it, giving a real parent–helper relationship a material of its own. Changing shape never means a bug has been fixed.

### Moss · 苔盒 — Long tasks & background care

A squat terracotta creature carrying a tiny garden on its back. An uneven patch of moss follows its chunky body, four sturdy legs anchor it, and its eyes sit inside shallow clay recesses. The garden belongs to the creature, right down to its little roots.

Moss moves slowly and steadily. Leaves hold pages, roots handle small objects, and its design story leaves room for a tentative new shoot during a quiet pause. Reliable and unhurried, it feels at home with work that needs time. Leaf growth and unfurling are expressions of character, never a measure of task progress.

### Thread · 线团 — Design & creative exploration

A coral-orange yarn creature with a pear-shaped body and the irregular charm of something made by hand. One ear is a loop, the other a loose end; its limbs are slightly different lengths. Its personality comes through tension, texture and posture.

Thread thinks by pulling a strand into shapes, adjusting a curve and trying another knot. Its story includes a beautiful knot at a moment of inspiration—and occasionally getting tangled in its own enthusiasm before calmly untangling. During an observed error, it stops pulling and examines the visible knot; the error animation does not untie it automatically. Loose strands stay within its own space.

### Glow · 灯籽 — Coordination & reminders

A translucent amber seed, narrow at the top and broad at the base, resting on four tiny supports. A warm speck of light hangs inside. Its expression comes from that inner light and the way it moves through the seed's clear body.

Gentle and restrained, Glow makes a natural lead-companion personality. Its design arranges light points in a quiet rhythm when coordinating, offers a single soft brightening for a reminder, and lets the light settle low when resting. It holds attention through precise movement and warm translucency, without a screen face, antenna or mechanical arms.

Main-agent appearances are assigned automatically. The first five families each receive a different base design; additional families use color variants. Every helper inherits its real root's **shape and color at a smaller size**, and existing families keep their appearance as others arrive or leave.

The character concepts and reference were supplied by the user. Sprite sheets were made with AI-assisted image generation, then integrated with project-authored rendering and motion. [Read the bilingual provenance statement](ORIGINALITY.md).

## 550 ways to be at work

**5 companions × 11 observed activities × 10 distinct performances.** Each material gets its own props, contact sequence and movement: paper folds and feeds, roots touch leaf keypads, yarn threads through small mechanisms, and light follows translucent tracks.

Reading · Searching · Planning · Coding · Designing · Testing · Building · Running · Waiting for tools · Waiting for you · Error

Coding keeps a recognizable input action; building assembles parts; running maintains an already-started mechanism. Waiting stops the work. Errors keep a visible jam, break or knot without pretending it has been repaired. A confirmed tool success gets a small, deduplicated response; completion requires actual completion evidence.

Open **Room preferences → Workshop companions → Motion gallery** to explore every character, activity and variation, with play/pause controls. [Browse public animation previews](docs/animations/README.md).

## A room that respects the work

- **One room for many real sessions.** Follow work across connected AI tools without losing session IDs or parent relationships. Helpers emerge from their observed parent, walk to work and return to that parent; nested families stay recognizable.
- **Read activity through movement.** Eleven observed activities have distinct actions: thinking tries combinations, coding inputs, building assembles and running keeps a mechanism moving. Waiting for a tool looks different from waiting for you. Inspect all 550 variations in the Motion gallery.
- **Keep everyone readable.** Choose a fixed companion size from 65% to 135%, with matching nameplates and smaller helpers. Adding agents never shrinks the room's cast. Moving agents may cross; settled work positions keep complete bodies and nameplates separate. If a chosen size cannot safely admit a new body, it waits for floor space and remains in the roster.
- **Find the right companion quickly.** Press **⌘K** to search, then inspect its real task, identity, current activity and ancestry. Main companions show active and cumulative helper counts, so a busy family is easy to follow.
- **See local activity and usage.** Review observation history, filter by date, tool or main/helper identity, and export observed activity as CSV. Daily Token and Weekly usage bring supported local token records into view, with model breakdowns in usage history. [What is measured](office/docs/monitoring.md).
- **Answer when an agent needs you.** Supported OpenCode and MiMo Code questions can receive a manual answer in the studio. An answer is sent only after your explicit submission; tool approvals stay in the original tool. [Supported question versions](office/docs/monitoring.md#manual-native-questions).
- **Make the room yours.** Six everyday rooms and 27 holiday rooms offer distinct architecture and layouts. Follow the Hong Kong calendar, choose a room yourself, watch daylight turn to night, or preview a time of day. Switch between **English and Chinese** while original task names and IDs stay intact. [Explore the rooms](docs/rooms/README.md).
- **A little life around the work.** Furniture responds to genuine arrivals when there is room. Twelve pair interactions, small greetings and optional decorative visitors make the studio feel inhabited without inventing AI activity.
- **Comfort for a whole workday.** Reduced motion and low-power modes keep calm, informative poses. Hiding the window pauses animation clocks, and routine metadata refreshes preserve playback instead of restarting it.

## Supported AI tools

The connection catalog includes **28 integrations**, grouped below by their observation entry point. Open **Connect AI tools** to find your tool and connect it; **Initialize** detects installed tools and sets up owned observation configuration after your click.

- **Hooks and local logs:** Codex CLI. Its bounded local rollout observer complements hooks, including real multi-agent relationships.
- **Hooks:** Claude Code, Copilot CLI, Gemini CLI, Antigravity CLI, Cursor Agent, CodeBuddy, Kiro CLI, KiroCrew, Kimi Code, Qwen Code, ZCode, CodeWhale, Qoder, Reasonix, QoderWork, QwenWork, WorkBuddy, TraeCode, Grok Build and MiniMax Code.
- **Plugins:** OpenCode, MiMo Code, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent and DeepSeek Harness **(experimental)**.
- **Extensions:** Pi and OMP.

These are observation integrations, with available events depending on the host and version. All 28 have isolated configuration and event regression coverage; real multi-agent observation has been verified with Codex, while other clients still need real-session acceptance checks. See the [compatibility guide](office/docs/upstream-compatibility.md) for host-specific details.

**Local token accounting covers seven tools:** Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Pi, OMP, Kimi Code and OpenCode. It reads supported local formats, can import usage recorded while the studio was closed, and keeps historical usage separate from live room activity. Totals reflect what the logs report; they do not estimate cost or subscription limits. [Monitoring coverage](office/docs/monitoring.md#actual-coverage).

**Manual native questions** are available for supported **OpenCode 1.x versions from 1.18.31** and **MiMo Code 0.1.x versions from 0.1.15**. Other tools and unsupported versions keep their questions in the original client. [Question behavior and limits](office/docs/monitoring.md#manual-native-questions).

## Local observation, explicit connections

The studio reads supported local session logs and events from hooks or plugins you choose to connect. It needs no cloud account, AI API key or telemetry service. Settings and observation history stay in the app's local data directory; the detailed storage boundaries are documented in the [app guide](office/README.md#local-data-and-credits).

Connections are explicit: the app installs or updates its observation configuration only when you choose to connect or initialize a tool. It preserves existing configuration and native tool approvals. The studio observes work; decorative motion never makes a task succeed, and no automatic answer or ordinary AI chat message is sent.

## Get v0.24.0

**macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon / arm64.** This is the currently verified target. The release includes a self-contained Electron runtime, so installed users do not need Node.js or a development server.

1. Open the [v0.24.0 Release page](https://github.com/Ye-0413/AI-Office-Workspace/releases/tag/v0.24.0) and download the arm64 DMG or ZIP.
2. With the DMG, drag **AI Mini Office.app** into **Applications**; with the ZIP, extract it and move the app there.
3. Open the app, connect the AI tools you use, then produce a fresh event in the original tool.

The published v0.24.0 downloads retain the name **AI Mini Office.app**. New builds from the current source use **AI Work Space.app**; the existing release files have not been renamed.

The current distribution uses local ad-hoc signing and is not Apple-notarized. macOS may display an unidentified-developer warning; review the release source and checksum before deciding whether to open it. This project does not require disabling system security protections.

## Run from source

The application lives in **`office/`**. The root package belongs to the retained upstream reference project. For development, use macOS, Node.js 24+ and Xcode Command Line Tools for the native helper.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/Ye-0413/AI-Office-Workspace.git
cd AI-Office-Workspace
npm --prefix office ci
npm --prefix office test
npm --prefix office start
```

```sh
# Native renderer smoke check
npm --prefix office run verify:renderer
# Local .app, DMG and ZIP
npm --prefix office run build:mac
```

Builds are written to `dist-mini-office/`, with the app named **AI Work Space.app**. The five workshop companions are included in the public source; they do not require restoring upstream character artwork. Legacy artwork may be prepared only from an authorized local checkout, using the optional procedure in the [app guide](office/README.md#公开源码版准备).
