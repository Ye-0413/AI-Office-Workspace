## Meet the workshop

Five materials, five silhouettes, five ways of paying attention. These portraits describe the **character concepts**, including ideas for signature gestures; the [Motion gallery](#550-ways-to-be-at-work) shows the motions implemented in this release. Their work themes give them personality. Every companion can represent every supported activity, and appearance does not route tasks or change an AI tool's abilities.

### Folio · 纸团 — Writing & research

A cream-white origami creature, shaped like a letter that has not quite finished folding itself. Two uneven paper corners form its ears; four short folded legs hold it up. Its face peeks out from the creases, as if it has always belonged there.

Folio is earnest, sensitive and a little too particular about a clean page. Its movement language is folding, turning and pressing flat: opening gently to write, folding one ear and then the other while thinking. In its character story, a finished piece emerges as one neatly prepared slip of paper—a small reward for getting the details right.

### Ink · 墨豆 — Coding & debugging

A nearly black navy ink drop with the low, flattened shape of a smooth stone that can flow. Four soft feet keep it close to the ground; one fine, luminous eye slit gives it a quietly determined expression.

Quiet, stubborn and quick to act, Ink works with fast little taps and compact changes of shape. Its design imagines a difficult bug flattening it into a small puddle before it gathers itself again. Helpers drip out of the same ink and merge back into it, giving a real parent–helper relationship a material of its own. Changing shape never means a bug has been fixed.

### Moss · 苔盒 — Long tasks & background care

A squat terracotta creature carrying a tiny garden on its back. An uneven patch of moss follows its chunky body, four sturdy legs anchor it, and its eyes sit inside shallow clay recesses. The garden belongs to the creature, right down to its little roots.

Moss moves slowly and steadily. Leaves hold pages, roots handle small objects, and its design story leaves room for a tentative new shoot during a quiet pause. Reliable and unhurried, it feels at home with work that needs time. Leaf growth and unfurling are expressions of character, never a measure of task progress.

### Thread · 线团 — Design & creative exploration

A coral-orange yarn creature with a pear-shaped body and the irregular charm of something made by hand. One ear is a loop, the other a loose end; its limbs are slightly different lengths. Its personality comes through tension, texture and posture.

Thread thinks by pulling a strand into shapes, adjusting a curve and trying another knot. Its story includes a beautiful knot at a moment of inspiration—and occasionally getting tangled in its own enthusiasm before calmly untangling. During an observed error, it stops pulling and examines the visible knot; the error animation does not untie it automatically. Loose strands stay within its own space.

### Glow · 灯籽 — Coordination & reminders

A translucent amber seed, narrow at the top and broad at the base, resting on four tiny supports. A warm speck of light hangs inside. Its expression comes from that inner light and the way it moves through the seed's clear body.

Gentle and restrained, Glow makes a natural lead-companion personality. Its design arranges light points in a quiet rhythm when coordinating, offers a single soft brightening for a reminder, and lets the light settle low when resting. It holds attention through precise movement and warm translucency, without a screen face, antenna or mechanical arms.

Main-agent appearances are assigned automatically. The first five families each receive a different base design; additional families use color variants. Every helper inherits its real root's **shape and color at a smaller size**, and existing families keep their appearance as others arrive or leave.

The character concepts and reference were supplied by the user. Sprite sheets were made with AI-assisted image generation, then integrated with project-authored rendering and motion. [Read the bilingual provenance statement](../ORIGINALITY.md).

## 550 ways to be at work

**5 companions × 11 observed activities × 10 distinct performances.** Each material gets its own props, contact sequence and movement: paper folds and feeds, roots touch leaf keypads, yarn threads through small mechanisms, and light follows translucent tracks.

Reading · Searching · Planning · Coding · Designing · Testing · Building · Running · Waiting for tools · Waiting for you · Error

Coding keeps a recognizable input action; building assembles parts; running maintains an already-started mechanism. Waiting stops the work. Errors keep a visible jam, break or knot without pretending it has been repaired. A confirmed tool success gets a small, deduplicated response; completion requires actual completion evidence.

Open **Room preferences → Workshop companions → Motion gallery** to explore every character, activity and variation, with play/pause controls. [Browse public animation previews](animations/README.md).

## 五位伙伴，五种性格

### 01 · 纸团 FOLIO — 文案与资料整理

**一页也认真。** 奶油白的折纸小兽，身体像一封还没完全折好的信。两个不对称的纸角是耳朵，四条短短的折纸腿撑住身体，脸藏在层层折痕之间。

它的动作语言是**折叠、翻页、压平**：写作时轻轻展开身体，思考时两只耳角交替折起；完成后的标志性画面，是从身体里抽出一张整齐的小纸条。认真、敏感，还有一点可爱的完美主义，像那个总会替你再检查一遍段落的小伙伴。

### 02 · 墨豆 INK — 编程与排错

**安静，行动快。** 深蓝近黑的扁圆墨滴，低低趴着，四只软脚贴近地面，一道细亮的眼缝藏在轮廓里。它像一颗会流动的小石子，沉默，却很有主意。

它用**流动、伸缩、聚散**表达工作：前脚快速敲键，遇到难题时缩成一小滩，再慢慢鼓起来。小分身从身体里“滴”出去，归返时重新融入主身。安静、倔强，遇到 bug 会停下来盯住那个故障点。

### 03 · 苔盒 MOSS — 长任务与后台维护

**慢慢来，也稳稳地向前。** 矮方形的陶土小兽，四条粗短腿站得很稳，眼睛嵌在陶土凹槽里。背上覆盖着不规则的苔藓与叶片，像一只背着小花园的生物。

它用**叶片、短根、托举**完成动作：稳稳走到工位，托起书页，压实模块；设定中，背上的叶片会舒展，等待时偶尔探出一片新芽。可靠、不着急，很适合长久陪伴。叶片的生长表达性格，实际任务进度仍以工具信息为准。

### 04 · 线团 THREAD — 设计与创意探索

**把想法绕成形。** 珊瑚橙的毛线小兽，身体偏梨形，一只耳朵是线圈，另一只是松散的线头。四肢长短略有差异，保留着亲手编织般的不规整与弹性。

它的动作语言是**拉线、绕形、编织、解结**：思考时把线头绕成不同轮廓，灵感来了，就收成一个漂亮的小结。角色故事里，它偶尔会把自己缠住，等理清思路，再若无其事地解开。活泼、有手作感，连试错都带一点小幽默；真实异常状态会保留线结，等待实际修复证据。

### 05 · 灯籽 GLOW — 协调与提醒

**需要你时，轻轻亮一下。** 半透明的琥珀色种子，上窄下宽，底部有四个小支点，内部悬着一粒暖光。它的表情藏在透光的身体和光点的位置里，轮廓安静而柔和。

它用**内光移动、光点排列、透光**表达状态：协调时光点有节奏地移动，提醒时克制地亮一下，休息时暖光沉到身体底部。温和、精确，适合作为主助手的性格原型，让等待回应也能保持从容。

**一个家族，一眼认出。** 主 Agent 随机获得一种角色；前五个家族使用五种不同的原色造型，更多家族复用造型并获得配色变体。同一场景内，主 Agent 的“造型＋配色”各不相同。分身继承真实根家族的外观，体型更小；在场家族不会因为新伙伴加入或离开而换造型。

五位角色来自用户提供的概念与参考，使用 AI 辅助生成姿态图，再由项目实现渲染、动作编排与状态连接。[查看中英双语原创声明](../ORIGINALITY.md)。

## 550 套动作，让忙碌有细节

**5 位伙伴 × 11 类真实活动 × 每类 10 套动作。** 各自使用符合材质的道具、接触顺序和运动路径：纸张折叠与送入，根须敲打叶片键盘，毛线穿过小机构，暖光沿透明轨道运行。

阅读 · 搜索 · 思考规划 · 编程 · 设计 · 测试 · 构建 · 执行 · 等待工具 · 等待用户 · 异常

编程保持持续输入，构建组装结构，执行表现已启动的运行回路。等待时工具停下来；异常保留卡纸、断路、断根或线结，不假装已经修好。单次工具成功只有轻微、去重的回应；整轮完成需要真实完成证据。

打开 **房间偏好 → 工坊伙伴 → 动作图鉴**，可逐一选择角色、活动与动作，播放或暂停查看。[浏览公开动画预览](animations/README.md)。
