# AI Work Space v0.25.1

- **Less crowding.** A family with its main agent present shows up to three active helpers by default. The main agent’s nameplate counts folded helpers. The roster keeps each task searchable and lets you expand the helpers that fit in the room.
- **Consistent family appearance.** Helpers share their main agent’s character, colours and family number. If a helper arrives first, its reported ancestry determines the group.
- **Safer configuration locks.** A live process keeps its lock even during a long operation. The app still recovers abandoned locks.
- **Original workshop characters.** The five Pixel and Painted companions are included. The retired Clawd and capsule robot renderers and their artwork import path have been removed.

Download the **Apple Silicon** DMG or ZIP and move **AI Work Space.app** into Applications. The app targets **macOS 13 or later** and uses the existing AI Mini Office data directory. These packages use ad-hoc signing. They ship without Apple notarization. Verify either file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## 简体中文

- **房间更清楚。** 主角色在场时，每组默认最多显示三位活跃分身，其余计入主角色名牌。伙伴名单可以搜索每项任务，并按可用工位展开分身。
- **家族外观一致。** 分身沿用主角色的造型、配色和家族编号。分身先到时，也会按已知父子关系归组。
- **长时间运行更稳定。** 持锁进程仍在运行时，配置锁不会因持有时间过长而被误回收。
- **保留五位原创工坊伙伴。** 像素风与手绘风继续可用。旧版 Clawd 和胶囊机器人渲染及素材导入入口已移除。

下载适用于 **Apple Silicon 和 macOS 13 以上**的 DMG 或 ZIP，将应用移入「应用程序」。本版沿用原有的 AI Mini Office 数据目录。安装包采用本地 ad-hoc 签名，尚未经过 Apple 公证。可用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对下载文件。
